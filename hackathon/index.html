About the challenge

Challenges you understand. Problems that matter.

Every track starts from a real quantum use case — assessed for technical maturity, matched against its classical baseline, and chosen for its relevance to Serbia’s industry and research strengths. You’ll know exactly what you’re solving, why the problem matters, and which quantum tools apply. No deep quantum training required to enter: each challenge comes with beginner-friendly framing, recommended tools, and clear judging criteria. Plan on Qiskit — it’s the framework for the whole event, resources are shared ahead of time, and the Fall Fest lecture day the weekend before gets you comfortable with it.
